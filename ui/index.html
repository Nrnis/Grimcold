Support Grimcold

Grimcold is free and stays free. It is an unofficial fan tool made in spare time; if it is useful to you, you can support its development with a donation.

A donation is a voluntary gift: it does not unlock anything or give perks, and everyone gets the same app.

Report a bug

Tell us what happened

The best report is a short form on GitHub (a free account is needed): what you did, what you saw, what you expected. The app fills in its version and the details below for you.

Send an anonymous technical report

The app's version, Windows, the graphics card and its driver, the errors seen and the log's last lines. No files, no names, no email and nothing typed by you. Stored in the EU for 30 days, used only to fix bugs.

This build sends no reports by itself: copy the details into the GitHub form instead.
