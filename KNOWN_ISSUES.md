# Known issues

Already known, no need to report them again (a new detail about one is welcome).

## Maps

- About 250 objects whose model could not be found are left out (fuji's light poles and bridge lamps,
  newyear_foray's big poles, some of castle's brick walls).
- Spotlights light a round cone, without the game's light patterns.
- The water's own ripples, splashes and wakes (hits on water, propellers, a cannon's blast over the
  water) are not shown.
- tower: a dark band along the horizon; the high-rises' broken top floors are lit by the lamps inside.
- Colours and light that still differ from the game: canyon cliffs too bright (cityruin), fuji's red
  tower and orange monorail pale, too little snow on geopp, grass thinner than the game's, far fog too
  thin on newyear_foray and tower, the backlit spruce on bridge too dark.

## Vehicle Builder

- Left and right L-shaped corner frames are not told apart.
- Engine sounds are not matched to each cabin.
- Jiggle bones (antennas, chains) do not swing.
